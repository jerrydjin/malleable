## What we learned

Zero benefit can pass a cost-only bar: the Potrero queue-yield experiment's
**0 s** gain did. Conversely, the guarded broadcast's **13.18 s** mean gain
cost a driver **575.75 s**, so benefit alone does not establish success.
The shared-record trust boundary held, while the NEAR control passed on
**8/10 seeds** and delivered labour priority still gained **0 s**.
The subsequent **5/10** labour-validity result and the freeway baseline's
**1,938 overlaps on seed 1** identify prerequisites for further efficacy tests;
neither STOP is evidence that the record gets drivers through faster.
